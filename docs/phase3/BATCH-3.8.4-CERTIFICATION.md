## 3. Invariant I22 + 15 Subordinate Constitutional Laws (I22-A through I22-O)

Batch 3.8.4 formally codifies and enforces **Invariant I22** and its 15 subordinate constitutional laws:

> **Invariant I22 — Scenario Sovereignty:**  
> **Charlie may simulate alternative futures, but a simulation must never be mistaken for reality, causal proof, approval, or execution authority.**

| Law Code | Law Title | Operational Contract Requirement | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I22** | **Scenario Sovereignty** | $\text{Scenario} \neq \text{Reality}$. Simulating an outcome never alters current business state or reality ledger. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-A** | **Counterfactual $\neq$ Historical Fact** | Counterfactual reasoning ("what would have happened if...") is epistemic simulation, never historical ground truth. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-B** | **Simulation $\neq$ Experiment** | Mathematical simulation is not an empirical real-world experiment; confidence bounded accordingly. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-C** | **Simulation $\neq$ Causal Proof** | Simulating a counterfactual path does not prove causation without structural identifiability in 3.8.1 DAG. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-D** | **Scenario Output $\neq$ Forecast Fact** | Scenario projections depend on active hypothetical assumptions; they cannot overwrite calibrated forecasts. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-E** | **Explicit Assumption Hashing** | Every assumption is declared, immutable, typed, and included in the SHA-256 scenario hash. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-F** | **Mandatory Uncertainty Preservation** | Single point estimates forbidden; every outcome produces P10, P50, P90 percentiles and sensitivity radius. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-G** | **Constraint Pre-Flight Gating** | Scenarios violating capital, liquidity, regulatory, or policy constraints marked `Constrained` or `Infeasible`. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-H** | **Deterministic Comparison** | Multi-scenario ranking uses Pareto dominance, net risk-adjusted return, and minimax regret. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-I** | **Deterministic Validation of AI Scenarios** | If LLM proposes a scenario, schema, parameter bounds, and invariants are deterministically verified. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-J** | **Zero Execution Authority** | Emits ZERO `ExecutionPermit`, dispatches NO tasks, calls NO external tools, and mutates NO accounts. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-K** | **Bidirectional Provenance** | Full cryptographic linkage: `Reality Observation -> Causal Hypothesis -> Forecast -> Radar -> Scenario`. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-L** | **Tenant Isolation** | Scenarios and simulated states strictly isolated by `TenantId`. Cross-tenant data contamination blocked. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-M** | **Reproducible Simulation** | Identical inputs, assumptions, policy version, and RNG seed yield bit-for-bit identical outputs. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-N** | **Model & Policy Immutability** | Snapshots of active policies, coefficients, constraints, and models frozen at simulation time. | **ENFORCED** |
| **I22-O** | **UNKNOWN Epistemic Propagation** | If baseline metrics or interventions rely on ungrounded assumptions, the output is flagged `Unknown`. | **ENFORCED** |
