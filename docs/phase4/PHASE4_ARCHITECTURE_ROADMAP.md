## 2. Phase 4 Constitutional Invariants

Phase 4 codifies five primary constitutional invariants (`I36` through `I40`):

### Invariant I36 — Executive Cognitive State Sovereignty (Batch 4.0)
$$\boxed{\text{INTELLIGENCE} \ne \text{AWARENESS} \ne \text{RESPONSIBILITY} \ne \text{DECISION} \ne \text{AUTHORITY} \ne \text{EXECUTION}}$$
- Raw intelligence (models, embeddings, metrics) does not equal executive awareness.
- Awareness does not automatically confer responsibility.
- Responsibility formulates decisions, but decisions do not constitute authority.
- Authority requires PRG-1 human governance before any real-world execution occurs.

### Invariant I37 — Bounded Responsibility Cycle Sovereignty (Batch 4.1)
$$\boxed{\text{CONTINUOUS} \ne \text{UNBOUNDED} \ne \text{UNSUPERVISED} \ne \text{STATELESS}}$$
- Autonomous operation is achieved via discrete, checkpointed **Durable Responsibility Cycles**, never unbounded infinite loops.
- Every cycle has an explicit state snapshot, bounded reasoning budget, auditable proposal output, and deterministic checkpoint.

### Invariant I38 — Multimodal Perception $\ne$ Execution Authority (Batch 4.2)
$$\boxed{\text{PERCEPTION} \ne \text{INTERPRETATION} \ne \text{INTENT} \ne \text{AUTHORITY} \ne \text{ACTION}}$$
- Visual perception (screen captures, OCR, document reading, camera feeds) is raw observational input.
- A visual artifact stating "Click Approve" or "Send Payment" is data, never an authorization.
- All actions executed by computer agents must route through the Batch 6 Execution Firewall with explicit permits.

### Invariant I39 — Omnichannel Identity Convergence (Batch 4.3)
$$\boxed{\text{CHANNEL} \ne \text{IDENTITY} \ne \text{AUTHORITY}}$$
- Charlie possesses exactly one unified identity and governance kernel regardless of communication medium (Web, Mobile, Voice, Email, Messaging, API).
- Channel-specific agents (e.g. "WhatsApp Charlie") are prohibited from establishing separate authority or unmetered capabilities.

### Invariant I40 — Operational & Commercial Firewall Invariance (Batches 4.4–4.7)
$$\boxed{\text{COMMERCIAL PROPOSAL} \ne \text{COMMERCIAL COMMITMENT} \ne \text{DISBURSEMENT} \ne \text{LEGAL OBLIGATION}}$$
- CRM outreach, pricing adjustments, supplier purchase orders, and financial disbursements remain governed proposals until validated by organizational policy and human sign-off.

## 6. Forbidden Authority Paths (Architectural Dead-Ends)

The following 10 implementation antipatterns are **strictly forbidden** in Phase 4:

1. **The Unbounded Daemon (`while(true)`)**: Continuous execution without durable checkpoints, timeout budgets, and human pause capabilities.
2. **Perception-to-Execution Short Circuit**: A multimodal vision model detecting a button on a web page and issuing direct clicks without Worker Fabric verification and Batch 6 permits.
3. **Fragmented Channel Personalities**: Creating disconnected bots for WhatsApp, Slack, or Email with divergent memory, state, or authority.
4. **Autonomous Commercial Commitment**: AI agents signing contracts, making legally binding representations, or changing contractual terms without human authorization.
5. **Direct Ledger Modification**: Finance or operations modules mutating general ledger balances or inventory records without double-entry audit records.
6. **Bypassing OARA via Emergency Flags**: Giving sales or operations modules a "critical" flag that overrides lexicographic capacity constraints.
7. **Simulation Bleed**: Digital twin states leaking into production operational databases or being presented as live truth without epistemic tags.
8. **Unchecked Adaptation Promotion**: Operational feedback loops silently adjusting risk floors, spend ceilings, or safety parameters.
9. **Cross-Tenant Data Blending**: Using multi-tenant data for shared model fine-tuning or cross-tenant prompt contamination.
10. **Self-Escalating Permissions**: An agent dynamically requesting, approving, and utilizing elevated execution permissions.

## 7. AI & Agent Boundaries

- **Role-Based Agent Specialization**: Agents within Charlie are dedicated workers (e.g. `CRMWorker`, `SupplyChainWorker`, `FinanceAnalystWorker`), operating under strict capability contracts defined in Batch 3.7 Capability Factory.
- **Stateless Agent Inference**: Agents do not maintain private hidden state across cycles; all working memory must be committed to 3.9.3 Organizational Memory or the Cycle State Snapshot.
- **Hallucination Containment**: Model outputs containing non-deterministic assertions must be verified against the Enterprise Digital Twin or Empirical Reality before admission as evidence.
- **Provider Neutrality**: Multi-model routing (via 3.0 OmniRoute) remains intact. No single LLM provider owns the executive cognitive state.

## 10. Multi-Tenant SaaS & Enterprise Scale Architecture

- **Tenant Isolation**: Every database query, cache key, vector index, and worker queue is strictly qualified by `TenantId`.
- **Resource Quotas**: Hard rate limits, token budgets, and concurrency caps enforced at the tenant level.
- **Data Residency**: Regional database clustering and storage isolation for compliance with GDPR, DPDP, and SOC 2.
- **Role-Based & Attribute-Based Access Control (RBAC/ABAC)**: Granular permissions for Executive, Operations, Finance, Sales, and Auditor personas.

## 11. Security, Compliance & Adversarial Hardening

Phase 4 mandates compliance with industry standards and defense against 20+ adversarial attack vectors:

- **Adversarial Input Defense**: Indirect prompt injection, jailbreaking, and visual prompt injection in multimodal perception are sanitized before brain ingestion.
- **Cryptographic Audit Trails**: All decisions, approvals, and executed actions maintain immutable SHA-256 hashes in append-only storage.
- **Secret Management**: Zero hardcoded credentials or model-accessible API keys; all external connections use enterprise secret vaults.
- **Fail-Closed Default**: Any system error, timeout, or security anomaly defaults to an immediate fail-closed state.

## 13. What Must NEVER Become Autonomous (The Immutable Negative Scope)

To preserve corporate safety, legal integrity, and architectural purity, Charlie will **NEVER**:
1. ❌ Autonomously authorize financial disbursements or credit facilities above governed thresholds.
2. ❌ Autonomously execute legally binding contracts or commercial representations without human review.
3. ❌ Autonomously rewrite constitutional invariants, safety floors, or execution firewall policies.
4. ❌ Autonomously deploy unverified capabilities directly into production environments.
5. ❌ Autonomously bypass or forge PRG-1 human approval signatures.
6. ❌ Grant unmetered or unsandboxed execution rights to external subagents or browser workers.
7. ❌ Treat simulated outcomes as empirical facts for financial, tax, or legal reporting.
8. ❌ Allow cross-tenant data leakage or multi-tenant model weight pollution.
